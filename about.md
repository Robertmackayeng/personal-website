# About Robert MacKay, P.Eng.

## Professional Overview

I am a Professional Engineer (P.Eng.) with over 10 years of specialized experience in instrumentation, control systems, and automation engineering. My career has focused on designing and deploying mission-critical systems across nuclear energy, oil & gas, and petrochemical sectors.

Currently, I'm the **I&C Lead System Engineer** at **GE Vernova Hitachi Nuclear Energy**, leading control systems development for the BWRX-300 small modular reactor project at Ontario's Darlington Nuclear Power Plant.

## Core Competencies

**Control Systems & Automation**
- Distributed Control Systems (DCS) architecture and design
- PLC programming (Allen-Bradley ControlLogix, DeltaV)
- Process automation and control theory
- EtherNet/IP and advanced network protocols

**Nuclear Standards & Regulations**
- IEC 61513 (Nuclear Power Plant I&C Systems)
- CSA N299.3 compliance and nuclear quality standards
- Regulatory design processes and documentation

**Infrastructure & Design**
- Fiber optic design and deployment
- Instrumentation engineering and procurement
- Electrical systems and panel design
- Project commissioning and startup support

**Leadership & Project Delivery**
- Design and field engineering team leadership
- Multi-disciplinary project coordination
- Technical problem resolution and innovation
- Budget management and schedule execution
